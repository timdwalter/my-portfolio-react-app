# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Three audiences, all confirmed by Tim:

- **Potential clients for side projects** – companies or people who might hire Tim part-time for software, cloud or AI work. Tim is employed and not yet freelancing; side projects are an option he is open to.
- **Recruiters and employers** – evaluating Tim for a possible next role.
- **Professional network and readers** – colleagues, LinkedIn contacts and readers of his Medium articles.

They arrive mostly from LinkedIn, Medium or a direct link and want to understand quickly what Tim does, where he has done it, and whether he is credible.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of M.Eng. Tim D. Walter (brand: TimTech Solutions). It shows what he works on, the industries he has worked in, his projects and his writing.

**Success:** the visitor connects with Tim on LinkedIn. Email contact is secondary.

## Positioning

Technical Project Lead at Lufthansa Industry Solutions who still writes code: Java/cloud backend, AI engineering and agentic development – with experience in medical imaging, aviation and smart campus planning. (Confirmed by Tim.)

Refined by Tim:
- Primarily a **tech lead**, not a backend specialist.
- Works **full-stack** – these days everyone is, because coding is agentic.
- High standards; **selective about what is good enough**.
- Still knows what **real development** takes.
- Can **talk to clients, write proposals, and draw up schedules and budget plans**.
- Relies on **critical thinking**: asks critical questions about requirements, estimates and architecture; reviews AI-generated code as critically as everything else.
- Still builds a good part himself; **high quality matters more than fast output**.
- Home hero copy is Tim's own wording (EN); keep it.

## Operating Context

- Bilingual: English (default) and German (`/de`).
- Visitors often come from LinkedIn and Medium; the site links back to both.
- Hosted on Vercel; Next.js 16 Pages Router, React 18, Tailwind, TypeScript, Node 26. Content lives in `src/content/`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: home, projects (incl. an interactive Three.js demo), about (story, career, skills, certifications), writing (links to Medium), legal notice, privacy.
- German legal pages (Impressum, Datenschutz) must exist. The Impressum intentionally lists only name and email.
- Fonts must be self-hosted (GDPR); no third-party font CDNs.
- Tim is employed at LHIND; side-project availability is phrased as "open to selected projects", not as a freelance business.

## Brand Commitments

- Name shown as **M.Eng. Tim D. Walter**; brand **TimTech Solutions**; logo is a hexagon with a cube in three tones of the site accent (#8FBAC3, #0E6F86, #0A5C70); the earlier bright logo cyan #3DD8F5 is retired (Tim's decision).
- Voice: direct, sober, first person, short active sentences, no superlatives or marketing phrases.
- Goal stated by Tim: the site must not look like an AI-generated template. Binding design rules live in `CLAUDE.md`.

## Product Principles

1. Credibility through specifics: real employers, industries, certifications and articles instead of claims.
2. Respect a busy reader: what Tim does and where he did it must be clear within seconds.
3. One clear next step: connect on LinkedIn.
4. Nothing invented – missing information stays missing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, visible focus states, semantic HTML, reduced motion respected.
